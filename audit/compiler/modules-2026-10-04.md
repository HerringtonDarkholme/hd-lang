# Current Compiler: Complete Module Catalog

Status: observational inventory at `caac922a`, 2026-10-04. Nothing here is accepted behavior or an owner decision.

Scope: all 159 TypeScript modules and three WAT fragments under `src/`. Module responsibilities come from declared surfaces, implementations, and callers; the catalog does not claim exhaustive correctness review of every function.

The [architecture baseline](status-quo-2026-10-04.md) describes subsystem contracts. [Checker](checker-2026-10-04.md) and [emitter](emitter-2026-10-04.md) analyses explain the shared state and algorithms behind the larger tables.
