## FAIL and N/A causes

Only what the output shows.

**Pillar 1 failures**

- mistakes: 93.1% of programs give exactly one diagnostic (67 of 72); 88.9% give the expected code (64 of 72); 0 of 72 offer a fix-it; the largest diagnostic is 107 tokens (question-in-void-function).
- pathological deep-blocks and deep-parens: `nesting-too-deep` (the implementation limit) at scale 4 (deep-blocks) and scale 1 (deep-parens).
- pathological method-chain at 4x: `hd check` overflowed its stack and aborted.
- pathological large-enum-match growth exponent: 2.42 against 1.3 (83 ms at 2x, 338 ms at 4x).
- recheck-precision: a public signature edit rechecks 9 modules against 3.
- errors-per-run mixed: 2 of 10 mistakes reported (the file with 2 syntax errors).
- check-cost: checked 988 ms against unchecked 559 ms (1.77x).
- dev-speed (2): the optimized work is within noise (under 50 ms) for fib, string-build, map and sort, so the ratio is not judged and fails.

**Pillar 2 failures**

- startup, `hd check empty.hd` peak RSS: 13.8 MB against 10 MB. Its wall time sits at the 20 ms goal (18 to 21 ms across runs).
- concurrency at N = 16: p95 latency 2.4x to 2.9x of N = 1 against 1.71x (N = 4 sits at the 1.5x goal: 1.4x to 1.7x across runs).
- disk: 11.0 MB, the release binary; build artifacts are 6758 B.
- parallel-speedup: 1.9x with `HD_JOBS` 14 against 1, goal 4.8x. `hd check` rejects `--jobs`, although `hd help check` lists it.
- cache-contention, dependency cache, fetched once: 8 concurrent checks made 16 connections to the remote; one check makes 2.

**Pillar 3 failures**

- proptest-perf cases per second: 28k/s against 100k/s.
- integration-test-perf: 71 ms setup per program (20 ms), growth exponent 1.006 (needs under 1), 66 ms per doc test (20 ms).
- runtime: sum 4.77x Node (3x); string-build 3.18x (3x). The geomean, 1.35x, passes.
